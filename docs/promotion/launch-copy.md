## English Community Post

Title: When fiction dialogue reads like meeting minutes: a Codex skill with inspectable examples

I maintain Novel Writing, an MIT-licensed Codex skill for fiction planning, drafting, continuation, and revision.

The problem I'm focusing on isn't just flat wording. A scene can contain plenty of nods, glances, and equipment handling while the characters still take turns delivering information. A useful revision asks what the exchange makes someone do differently—and doesn't force an action tag onto every line.

The repository includes three original teaching cases:

- a sticky latch that turns a delivery argument into a repair decision;
- a folded letter that creates suspicion without proving a hidden motive;
- an umbrella handoff repaired without removing the narrator's defensive rhythm.

These are AI-assisted editorial illustrations, not measured model comparisons. Each has a source passage, a prompt, a diagnosis, and a possible revision. The skill also covers viewpoint access, character anchoring, causal outlines, and preserving style-bearing details.

If you try it, I'd welcome a specific failure: a sentence where it invents knowledge, flattens your voice, or adds actions that do no work. Use a short passage you can safely publish, or an invented reproduction; no need to share a private chapter.

## 中文长帖

标题：AI 写小说，对白像会议记录，加上“点头皱眉”就能救吗？

我在维护一个 MIT 开源的小说创作 skill：Novel Writing，面向 Codex。

它想解决的一个具体问题是：人物说了很多话，也操作了不少设备，但对话结束时，除了读者获得一批信息，场景里几乎什么都没发生。

比如一个为讲解专门编写的片段：主管说模型箱今天要交，学徒说锁扣会卡。给他们各加一个“点头”“皱眉”，分歧仍停在嘴上。另一种处理是：学徒把箱子压在出货单上，请主管亲手打开；锁扣真的卡住，他把单子抽回来，问换一个要多久。

关键不在动作多少，而在试开失败改变了接下来的交涉。同时，几句连着的短对白也可以不加动作，不需要把每次沉默都翻译成人物心理。

仓库还准备了两个案例：一个只让人物产生怀疑、不让她凭空知道秘密；另一个只修递伞动作，保留第一人称嘴硬的重复和没说破的关系。

需要说清楚：这些是 AI 辅助编写的讲解示例，不是同模型开关 skill 的对照实验，更不是所有小说都会变好的保证。它的用途是把审稿问题具体化，让修改方向可以讨论和检查。

如果你也用 Codex 写小说，欢迎试一小段。最有帮助的反馈不是一句“不错”，而是告诉我：哪句话越过了视角，哪个动作多余，哪处修改把你的语气改没了。可以用自己编的短片段复现，不要公开不该公开的整章稿件。
