# Viewpoint: Suspicion Is Not Knowledge

[All examples / 示例目录](README.md)

Original, AI-assisted editorial demonstration—not a measured model comparison. Context: Lin has arrived early to return a key to the rehearsal-room caretaker. She remains in the corridor. She cannot read the folded letter or enter his thoughts. Keep the letter's meaning unknown.

## Prompt

```text
Use novel-writing. Review and locally revise the source below in Lin's
limited viewpoint. She came early to return a key and stays in the corridor.
She can observe the caretaker, but cannot read his folded letter or know
his motive. Preserve suspicion without proving that anyone has betrayed her.
Do not invent a new source of information to justify the existing certainty.
Give concrete findings before the revision.
```

## Source

```text
Lin stopped outside the rehearsal room with the key in her palm. The
caretaker folded a letter and put it in his coat. He had accepted payment
to cancel her booking, and was afraid she would discover it.
"You're early," he said.
Lin knew he had sold her out. She slipped the key into her pocket and
asked who had written to him.
```

## Diagnosis

- **Location:** "He had accepted payment" and "was afraid." **Type:** cognition and viewpoint overreach. A visible folded letter becomes access to a hidden transaction and motive. **Revision:** stay with observed timing and movement; do not invent readable text. **Project rule?** Yes for this scene's specified limited viewpoint, not a ban on omniscient fiction.
- **Location:** "Lin knew." **Type:** epistemic status. Suspicion is promoted to certainty without evidence. **Revision:** let uncertainty affect a reversible choice, such as delaying the key handoff and asking a question. **Project rule?** Track claims against information available at that moment.

## One Possible Revision

```text
Lin stopped outside the rehearsal room with the key in her palm.

The caretaker looked up. The fold he was making in a letter went crooked;
he pressed it flat against his coat before putting it away.

"You're early."

She had meant to pass him the key through the doorway. She closed her
fingers around it instead.

"Bad time?"

"Not at all."

He held out his hand. She kept hers at her side.

"Is my booking still all right?"
```

The letter and awkward fold are observations. Their meaning is unresolved. Lin's hesitation affects the key handoff and the question she chooses, but neither the narrator nor the example declares her suspicion correct. The change removes the original hidden-motive claim; a larger story could reveal it later through evidence.

## 中文平行示例

场景约束：林岚提前来排练室归还钥匙，始终站在走廊。管理员收起一封折着的信；她看不到内容，也不知道他的真实动机。

**提示词**

```text
使用 novel-writing。先诊断，再局部改写下面片段。
坚持林岚的有限视角：她提前来还钥匙，站在走廊，读不到信。
保留疑心，但不能证明有人出卖她；不要凭空添加情报来源，
替原文不合理的确定判断找借口。让不确定性影响她下一步怎么做。
```

**原文**

> 林岚攥着钥匙停在排练室外。管理员把信折好，塞进外套。他已经收钱取消了她的预约，正怕被她发现。
>
> “来早了啊。”他说。
>
> 林岚知道自己被出卖了。她把钥匙放回口袋，问是谁给他写的信。

**一种修改**

> 林岚攥着钥匙停在排练室外。
>
> 管理员抬头，手里那道折痕压歪了。他把信在外套上按平，收进口袋。
>
> “来早了啊。”
>
> 她本来要把钥匙递进去，手指却又合上了。
>
> “不方便？”“哪儿的话。”
>
> 他伸着手。她的手还在身侧。
>
> “我订的时间，没变吧？”

“看到他收信”可以成立，“知道他收了钱”不行。修改保留疑心造成的停顿和试探，不替读者提前判定管理员有罪。换成有意设计的全知叙述时，应另按叙事约定审查。
